Selected work

A few projects, and what changed because of them.

NGO · Malaria prevention · Participatory SBCC

Malaria prevention messages, co-created with the communities they were for

We worked with caregivers, young people and local influencers to create materials that encourage consistent use of insecticide-treated nets and early treatment seeking.

Through community dialogues, story gathering and co-creation sessions, we turned what people told us into clear messages, illustrations and stories, checked against public health guidance.

People are more likely to trust and act on messages they see themselves in.

Business · Fashion · Brand identity & brand guide

Tisalewa: an identity for accessible, expressive fashion

We built the brand identity and brand guide for Tisalewa, a contemporary fashion brand making style accessible, expressive and comfortable.

The mark interlocks four T shapes into one emblem that also repeats as a pattern, so the brand carries across labels, packaging and prints.

Youth & advocacy · Mural · With Norsaac and Power to Youth

A mural in Tamale against sexual and gender-based violence

With Tamale's young people and local stakeholders, and support from Norsaac and the Power to Youth programme, the mural grew out of youth-led research, ideation and prototyping.

A year on, it is still sparking conversations about child marriage, teenage pregnancy and domestic violence, and stands as a permanent record of what young people in Tamale are calling for.
